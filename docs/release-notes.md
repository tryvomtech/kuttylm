**KuttyLM lets you chat with AI on your own computer — private and free.**
Your chats stay on your computer. It works offline once a model is downloaded.

**Why KuttyLM?** Many AI tools need a terminal, or are big apps full of technical settings.
KuttyLM is different: it is **lightweight**, it has **no commands — only buttons**, and it is
**made for people who are not technical**. It even tells you which models will run well on your computer.

## What's new in 0.2.1

## What's new in 0.2.0

- **Chat history:** your chats are saved on your computer and listed by date (Today, Yesterday,
  Previous 7 days …). Open an old chat and continue, search, rename or delete it.
- **AI titles:** each chat gets a short title written by the AI.
- **Nicer answers:** tables, lists, headings, quotes and code blocks (with a Copy button) are shown
  neatly. Links open in your web browser.
- **Your choice:** turn off "Save chat history" or delete all chats in **Settings**.

## Which file do I need?

Scroll down to **Assets** and download **one** file:

| Your computer | Download |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10 or 11** | **`KuttyLM-Windows-Setup.exe`** |
| **Mac with Apple chip** (M1, M2, M3, M4…) | **`KuttyLM-Mac.dmg`** |
| **Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04** | **`KuttyLM-Linux.deb`** |
| **Other Linux** | **`KuttyLM-Linux.AppImage`** |

You do **not** need the "Source code" files.

## First start

KuttyLM is not yet signed by Microsoft or Apple, so your computer asks once if you trust it.

- **Windows:** double-click the `.exe`. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**, then **Install**. If Windows asks to install "Microsoft Visual C++", click **Yes**.
- **Mac:** open the `.dmg` and drag KuttyLM into **Applications**. Open it once, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Ubuntu:** double-click the `.deb` and click **Install**.

## How to start

1. Open **Find models** and download a model marked **Fits well**. Try **SmolLM2 135M** (100 MB) first.
2. In **My models**, click **Run**.
3. Open **Chat** and ask a question.

## What your computer needs

- 8 GB of memory or more is best (small models work with 4 GB)
- Disk space for the models (0.1–5 GB each)
- Internet only to download KuttyLM and models
